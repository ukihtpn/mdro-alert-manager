MASTERLIST status

Not started — 0 field wajib terisi
Draft — some required fields filled
Complete — all required fields filled

MDRO form status (column AF)

Form complete — AF = Yes
Form pending — AF empty
To verify — filled, awaiting UKI confirmation

Overdue alerts

A case is flagged overdue when it stays incomplete for more than 7 days from the sample date. Two separate reminders: one for MASTERLIST, one for the MDRO form.

Roles

UKI: verify submitted forms, then resolve once complete & accurate.

Ward / Doctor: fill patient MDRO forms for their own ward, can save as draft.

Ward login

Each ward has its own username (lowercase ward code: w3, w7, icumb...) and its own password. When UKI taps "Send link", the ward username & password are included in the WhatsApp message.

The actual password is stored & checked in the "Users" tab of the Google Sheet (after GAS deploy) — not visible in the app code.
